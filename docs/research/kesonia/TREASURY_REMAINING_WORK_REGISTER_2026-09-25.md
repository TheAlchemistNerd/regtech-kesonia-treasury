### The gaps previously grouped too broadly

FRTB, LCR/NSFR and hedge accounting deserve distinct work packages. They should not disappear under the headings “capital”, “liquidity” and “IFRS integration”. The following details also need to be explicit inside the existing packages:

- **R04:** Hull–White or Black–Karasinski model choice, calibration evidence, prepayment-speed assumptions and the OAS solver; these are named in the paste but have not been implemented here.
- **R05–R07:** credit-spread sensitivity (CS01), hedge-counterparty dependence/wrong-way risk, bilateral versus cleared economics and eligibility of capital relief. A hedge's benefit must be calculated for the actual scope rather than assumed from its label.
- **R09:** software capability is only one step. The actual trade and accounting workflow still needs a demonstrated reconciliation, exceptions and restart behaviour.
- **R10:** economic protection, accounting recognition and prudential capital recognition require separate transaction-specific evidence. A dependence parameter or SPV diagram does not establish a regulatory capital benefit.
- **R13:** neural/Bayesian methods are already specified. Their incremental predictive and decision value remains an empirical question.

For the liquidity modules, Basel defines the LCR around a 30-day stress and separately specifies available and required stable funding for NSFR. Those requirements justify two distinct worked calculations. [Basel LCR](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/lcr?allChapters=true), [Basel NSF30](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-framework/standard/nsf/30/inforce/2019-12-15/published/2024-07-05). IFRS 9 hedge designation and documentation have their own qualifying requirements, supporting a dedicated accounting case. [IFRS Interpretations Committee, June 2019, application of IFRS 9 §6.4.1](https://www.ifrs.org/news-and-events/updates/ifric/2019/ifric-update-june-2019/).

## 3. What should be reused rather than presented as a new gap

- **Benchmark compounding versus a future term structure:** already explicit in the short KESONIA note and the earlier pricing review. The new casebook adds verification and examples.
- **Expected loss, pricing, ECL and capital distinctions:** already present in the controlled financial and loss specifications. The work now is reconciliation under common dates, exposures and scenarios.
- **Vintage analysis, default timing, cure and recovery:** existing research/protocol work and the controlled timing/loss sections address the design. Their empirical results remain a separate task. Preserve event definitions when connecting them to ALM cash flows.
- **SPV waterfall and protection design:** substantially specified in the controlled chapter. The treasury contribution is the populated scenario calculation and rate/behaviour interaction.
- **PAA/usage/refund distinctions:** already treated in the September 23 working formulations. A complete numeric accounting and settlement case remains useful.
- **Neural residualisation, shrinkage, Pólya–Gamma and dependence:** the controlled loss specification contains explicit methodological requirements. We should carry those requirements into experiments, not describe them as concepts absent from the project.
- **Gamma/negative-binomial and Bayesian relativities material:** preserve the user's established destination in the relativities paper. This review has not re-audited that full paper; it proposes an interface for its estimates and uncertainty rather than a duplicate derivation.

## 4. Priority for the next writing round

**First: strengthen the four KESONIA articles.** Develop R01–R03 and the core of R08–R09: the curve laboratory, dated funding calculation, behavioural assumptions, integrated scenario and controlled workflow. Start R18 alongside them so the examples use an explicit institutional and market scope. Include R16 in the integrated liquidity case and R17 wherever hedge accounting is claimed.

**Second: develop connected business cases.** R10, R11 and R14 connect the series to Mwendo, Underwrite to Collect, Owner Earnings and the workbench. The existing controlled specifications provide their financial boundaries.

**Third: build specialist appendices where the purpose warrants them.** R04–R07, R12 and R15 support option valuation, hedge design and capital analytics, including a distinct FRTB module. R13 follows the research programme's validation sequence. These modules can become substantial papers or laboratories; they need not all be inserted into the main KESONIA narrative.

This order preserves the user's broader aspirations. It does not limit the future series to these reviews, and it does not reopen the deferred cumulative screenshot catalogue.

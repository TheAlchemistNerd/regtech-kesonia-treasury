Implementing Automated ERP RegTech Sweep under the CBK KESONIA Framework
for Commercial Banks and Traditional Variable-Interest Lending
The transition to KESONIA is much more than a benchmark rate reform—it represents a
fundamental shift in how commercial banks and traditional lenders engineer pricing,
accounting, risk management, and regulatory compliance.
As institutions move from internal base rates and the Risk-Based Credit Pricing Model
(RBCPM) towards daily compounded-in-arrears reference rates, the challenge is no
longer simply calculating interest. The real challenge is building an enterprise architecture
capable of transforming daily market data into transparent, auditable, and regulator-ready
financial information.
My latest practitioner-focused article explores this transformation through the concept of an
Automated ERP RegTech Sweep—an enterprise workflow that integrates market data
ingestion, SQL-based interest computation, AI-assisted pricing, ERP accounting, automated
validation, and regulatory reporting into a single governance framework.
The article is written specifically for:
• Commercial banks
• Mortgage lenders
• Development Finance Institutions (DFIs)
• SACCO banking subsidiaries
• Traditional variable-interest lenders
• Chief Risk Officers (CROs)
• Chief Financial Officers (CFOs)
• Chief Information Officers (CIOs)
• ERP Solution Architects
• Enterprise Data Architects
• Regulatory Compliance Officers
• Banking Technology Consultants
Rather than approaching KESONIA solely from a treasury or quantitative finance
perspective, the article examines how enterprise systems become the system of regulatory
truth, bringing together core banking platforms, ERP financial modules, AI risk engines,
SQL processing pipelines, and automated supervisory reporting.
Some of the key areas covered include:
✔ Understanding why KESONIA follows the same backward-looking compounded-inarrears methodology as SOFR, SONIA, €STR, and SARON.
✔ Implementing scalable SQL architectures for daily reference-rate ingestion,
compounding, reconciliation, and auditability.
✔ Applying an AI-driven pricing framework where:
R = CCR + K(PD, LGD, EAD)

to separate transparent market pricing from institution-specific credit risk.
✔ Exploring why traditional multivariate normal correlation models underestimate systemic
banking stress and how the Clayton Copula better captures lower-tail dependence during
liquidity crises, exchange-rate shocks, and interest-rate spikes.
✔ Introducing the Automated ERP RegTech Sweep as a governance framework for
automated extraction, validation, reconciliation, encryption, digital signatures, workflow
approvals, immutable audit logging, and regulatory submission.
